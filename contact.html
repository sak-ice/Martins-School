Quick Answers
Frequently Asked Questions
How do I arrange a school tour? +

Simply fill in the contact form above, selecting "Book a Visit" as your subject. Our admissions team will follow up within one business day to confirm a date and time. Tours run every Tuesday and Thursday at 10:00am during term time.

How quickly will you respond to my enquiry? +

We aim to respond to all enquiries within one business day. For urgent matters, please call us directly on +234 703 990 9900 during office hours.

Can I speak with a current parent or student? +

Absolutely. During school tours we often arrange for prospective families to meet current parents and, where appropriate, students. Please mention this when booking your tour.

What are the fee payment options? +

Fees can be paid termly or in structured instalments. Contact our Bursary at bursary@martinsschool.edu.ng for a full fee schedule and payment plan options.

Is there a waiting list for oversubscribed year groups? +

Yes. When a year group is full, we maintain a waiting list and contact families as soon as a place becomes available. We recommend applying as early as possible to secure your child's place.
