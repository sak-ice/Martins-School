School Structure
Junior & Senior Secondary

Our secondary school is structured in two clear divisions, each with dedicated staff, timetables, and pastoral support.

JSS 1 – JSS 3

Junior Secondary

A broad, exploratory curriculum designed to build strong academic foundations across all subjects before students choose their specialisation.

English, Mathematics, Basic Science
Social Studies, CRS/IRS, Civic Education
Technology, Home Economics, Agriculture
French, Yoruba Language, Creative Arts
SS 1 – SS 3

Senior Secondary

Students choose one of three specialised streams in SS1, building depth and preparing for WAEC, NECO, and university admission.

🔬 Science Stream
Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Further Maths
💼 Commercial Stream
Economics, Commerce, Accounting, Data Processing
🎭 Arts & Humanities
Literature, Government, History, Fine Art

Enrichment & Extracurriculars

At Martins School, we believe the best students are well-rounded humans. Our extracurricular programme gives every student a place to shine.

⚽

Sports

Football, basketball, athletics, table tennis, and an annual inter-house sports competition.

🎭

Drama & Music

💬

Debate & Public Speaking

Award-winning debate team competing at state and national levels every year.

💻

Coding & Robotics

College & Career Readiness
Preparing for
What Comes Next

From JSS1 through SS3, our dedicated college counselling team supports each student in identifying their strengths, selecting the right universities, and crafting compelling applications.

Personal Mentorship

1-on-1 guidance with experienced counsellors from SS1 onwards — choosing courses, institutions, and career paths.

University Partnerships

Direct relationships with institutions across Nigeria, UK, US, and Canada — including campus visit days.

WAEC & NECO Preparation

Dedicated revision programmes, past paper workshops, and mock examinations from SS2.
