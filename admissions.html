Admissions Process
Five Simple Steps
1

Submit Your Application

Complete the online form with your child's details, academic records, and required documents. A non-refundable processing fee applies.

2

Entrance Assessment

Shortlisted applicants are invited for an age-appropriate academic and cognitive assessment. Montessori applicants have an observation session.

3

Family Interview

A friendly meeting with the Admissions Officer and/or Head of School to understand your child's needs, interests, and family values.

4

Admission Decision

Offers are sent within two weeks of the interview. A place is secured upon acceptance and payment of the required deposit.

5

Welcome to Martins School!

You'll receive an orientation pack, school calendar, uniform guide, and all you need for a smooth first day.

Required Documents
What to Prepare

Birth Certificate

Original or certified copy of the child's birth certificate.

Most Recent Academic Record

Latest school report or transcript (not required for Toddler).

Passport Photographs

Two recent passport-size photographs of the applicant.

School Reference Letter

A reference from the child's current or most recent school (Secondary applicants).

Medical / Health Information

Any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or special educational needs.

Frequently Asked Questions
Common Questions
What age does the Montessori programme start?
+
Our Montessori Toddler programme welcomes children from 18 months of age. Children may progress through to Primary (age 11).
How selective is admission to Secondary School?
+
Admission to our Secondary division is selective, based on academic records, the entrance assessment, and the family interview. We assess each applicant holistically — results are one part of a fuller picture.
Do you offer scholarships or bursaries?
+
Yes. A limited number of merit-based scholarships are available for exceptional Secondary applicants. Bursaries for financial need are considered on a case-by-case basis. Please contact the admissions office for details.
What is the class size?
+
Montessori classes have a maximum of 20 children. Junior Secondary classes are capped at 28 students, and Senior Secondary classes at 30. This ensures every student receives meaningful individual attention.
Is there school transport?
+
We operate a school bus service covering several zones in Ifo, including Aderenle, Oloshe, Coker Road, Vespa, and Ojusango Road. Contact us to confirm if your area is covered and to request a transport form.
Can we visit the school before applying?
+
Absolutely — we warmly encourage all prospective families to visit. We hold Open Days every term, and private tours can be arranged by contacting our admissions office. Seeing the school in person often answers questions no website can.
What if my child has special educational needs?
+
We are committed to inclusive education. Please indicate any learning differences or special needs on the application form. Our SEND co-ordinator will contact you to discuss how we can best support your child before and after admission.
How long does the admissions process take?
+
From submission of a complete application to offer letter, the process typically takes 4–6 weeks. Incomplete applications may take longer to process.
